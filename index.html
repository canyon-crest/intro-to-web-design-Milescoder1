Since a very young age, I have always been obsessed with leveraging mechatronic design to improve others’ lives. Some of my early inventions include a LEGO vending machine, a backyard roller coaster, and many intricate chain reactions. As my skills have evolved, so have my projects. Some of my recent projects include a thrust-vector thrust-vector controlled model rocket, a patent-pending outlet system, and a 3D-printed kickstand for Chromebooks with 500+ student users. To this day, I continue to push the envelope on the impact and complexity of my projects.

A Creative Jazz Keyboardist

I personally invest just as much time into my creative development as my analytical development. As a jazz pianist who has been playing for more than nine years, I deeply enjoy the art of improvisation and creative expression in a musical setting. From well-known standards to free jazz, I have explored a large range of literature and vocabulary over the years. A similar variety exists in the groups I have participated in, ranging from small small combos to big bands. The thrill of hearing split-second decisions combine into an emotionally moving performance is very addictive and I am sure this will be a lifelong obsession of mine.
